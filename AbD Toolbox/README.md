# Archiving by Design Toolbox

## About the Toolbox

The DLM Forum community has long been discussing how best to support the **Archiving by Design (AbD)** approach, also known as *Sustainable Access*, particularly through practical tools, guidance and supporting materials for AbD-related projects.

A key conclusion from these discussions is that **no single, universal methodology or comprehensive solution can adequately address the diversity of environments in which AbD is applied**. These environments differ in their legal and regulatory frameworks, institutional structures, cultural contexts, organisational practices and levels of AbD maturity.

At the same time, there is broad recognition that practical resources — such as tools, glossaries, checklists, assessment templates, requirement lists, risk management methods and workshop materials — can provide valuable support to the AbD community.

With this in mind, the **DLM Forum AbD Subforum** (formerly the EAG AbD Subgroup) has established the **AbD Toolbox**: a growing collection of practical tools and supporting materials developed by the expert members of the Subforum.

The Toolbox aims to make these resources **easy to find, understand, adapt and use**. Rather than prescribing a single approach, it offers a flexible collection of resources that organisations and practitioners can select and tailor to their specific needs and circumstances.
